A lead waits on a signal: a `SendMessage`, a task notification, its sweep's cron or the ops job already watching.
The ops jobs already watching are the merge train, ops-checks and ops-report's stalled-PR alerts.
A `sleep` loop or a repeated `gh` read for a state one of those brings costs a turn at the lead's full context, so it spends the account's week for nothing.
A signal that stops coming may be the Mac asleep, so read the sleep log before you name a gap in a relay, a tick or a sampled series, and leave the sleep out of any rate.
SESSIONS.md's Wait on a signal section holds the sleep-log read and how a watch script loops under zsh.
