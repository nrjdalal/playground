A new ticket in {tickets.tracker} waits for the owner's yes, posted as one Inbox `ticket` decision, and the factory's own work stays out of it.
A ticket speaks to the org in the owner's name, so the owner chooses each one, and the factory is the owner's own, so none of its work belongs there.
A session that needs one sends the team and the exact title up the chain; the dispatcher posts the decision (**ask**) and, on a yes, writes the approval the `no-linear-ticket` hook reads.
The ticket itself keeps to **classified**.
How the approval is written and checked: scripts/hooks/README.md, no-linear-ticket.
