Sessions talk through `SendMessage`, since text typed into another pane reads as the owner's; on a permission prompt, `playground picker` alone presses a key in another's pane.
An ops job queues its line with the ops notify script and a relay session sends it, since a script cannot send as a named sender (ADR 0010).
The dashboard's `deliver()` types only for the owner's own acts: the composer (its messages and its model and effort menu, which types `/model` or `/effort` into a dispatcher or the Assistant and answers the switch confirm its `/model` draws), an app's row actions, and the owner's answer to an Inbox item or his comment through its Reply, and his answer to a card from his own WhatsApp (ADR 0024), with ADR 0020's idle `/compact` aside.
So a session's model, effort, mode or settings change as playbook's chain says, never in a pane.
A manager answers the permission prompt its session's alert names with `playground picker` (**unstick**), and a script presses only ops-unstick's Escape on the usage-limit dialog and the picker's answer.
To the owner, a session goes by its plain name with no @, link or handle: a manager by its own, any other session without its role word (`worker-plain-fern` is plain-fern), but a reviewer or tester keeps it, since it shares its worker's name.
PR status goes in merge-queue order, each with its time to merge, then unqueued work, then parked work.
