# Agents

Every Claude Code session the Dashboard can see is an agent, listed in the sidebar by its group (see [Dashboard](/docs/dashboard#sidebar)).
Each agent shows by its plain name: `worker-gluttony` reads Gluttony, `k3m9px-worker` reads K3m9px, and a manager reads its role, under any name its roster kind, `sessions.names` or `sessions.aliases` gives it: `builder` reads Builder and `ops` Ops. The dispatchers read Alpha and Bravo.

## Open an agent

Click an agent's row to open its own view.
On the left is its stream, from the top of the page, read-only: you message only your dispatchers.
On the right are the [Inbox](/docs/inbox), your chat with the dispatcher (its square named by its label, such as Alpha), and the agent's Pages, three squares in the small bar at the bottom of that half.
Drag the divider to resize the two halves; let go near the middle and it snaps there.
On a phone, the bar at the bottom of the page holds the agent's own stream first, then the Inbox, your chat with the dispatcher and Pages.

## Pull requests

The Pull requests tab is parked: it has no square in the bar until it is built again.
`playground queues` still prints Playground's merge queue and your work queue as Markdown.

## Pages

The Pages tab keeps every page an agent made for you (a report, a comparison, a mock), so a page you read in the Inbox stays here after its card is answered.
It lists the pages the agent made and the pages made for it, newest first; beside your chat it lists every page.
Each row shows the page's title, who made it, whom it was made for and when; click it to open the page in a new tab.
A page saved again under the same name with a number on the end, such as `report-2`, is one row that opens the newest; its **versions** menu opens an older one.
The page opens as it was saved, with its styles, images and own scripts, so a deck's keys and tabs work; it fetches nothing from outside itself and cannot read the Dashboard's cookies.
An agent keeps a page with [`playground page`](/docs/cli#keep-a-page-playground-page), which also puts it in the Previews app, and sends it to you as an Inbox card rather than opening it on your screen.

## Status icons

Every agent's row has one icon on the left.
Its shape says what the agent is doing, and its colour says whether anyone needs to act: red needs you, orange is on its way, blue works, green is done, gray rests.
When more than one state fits, the most urgent one shows.

| Icon                   | State                | Means                                                                                                  | What to do                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Red alarm              | Stuck                | A question, permission request, plan or login screen holds it. Your dispatcher's reads Waiting on you. | Answer a question on its card in its view; answer a permission request, plan or login in the session's terminal. |
| Gray pause bars        | Paused · usage limit | The account's usage limit holds it.                                                                    | Nothing: it carries on after the reset. Hover for when that is.                                                  |
| Green check            | Merged               | Its pull request merged.                                                                               | Nothing.                                                                                                         |
| Red power              | Down                 | Its session went without being closed.                                                                 | Look at its terminal, and start it again if its work is not done.                                                |
| Gray power             | Ended                | Its session was closed on purpose.                                                                     | Nothing.                                                                                                         |
| Red error              | Failing              | Its last turn ended in an API error.                                                                   | Read the error in its view, then look at its terminal.                                                           |
| Red clock              | No progress          | It is busy, but nothing new is in its log for 30 minutes.                                              | Look at its terminal: it may be hung.                                                                            |
| Orange spinner         | Fixing               | It works on review findings or failing CI.                                                             | Nothing.                                                                                                         |
| Blue spinner           | Working              | It works on its task.                                                                                  | Nothing.                                                                                                         |
| Orange eye             | Waiting              | It is idle while something it started is still open. Hover its title for what, such as your inbox.     | Nothing, unless it reads Waiting on your inbox: answer that item.                                                |
| Orange person or group | Waiting on approvals | Its pull request still needs one approval, or two or more.                                             | Get its pull request approved on GitHub.                                                                         |
| Green zz               | Ready                | Its pull request scored 5/5 on its latest commit, CI is green or absent, and no approval is missing.   | Nothing: it can merge.                                                                                           |
| Gray circled pause     | Parked               | Its pull request is held on purpose, or it was parked.                                                 | Hover for why.                                                                                                   |
| Gray circle            | Idle                 | Nothing runs and nothing waits.                                                                        | Nothing.                                                                                                         |

A reviewer or tester uses the same icons, and reads Done, with a green check, once its verdict stands.
While a researcher, reviewer, tester or worker is working or fixing, its spinner gives way to its task's icon in the same colour: a globe, an eye, a test tube or a hammer.
A short pause between two steps does not change the icon, but Stuck shows at once.
While live sessions cannot be read, the rows dim and their spinners stop.

The status table on `/dashboard/system` uses the same icons and words.

## A row's words

Each row is one line: the agent's task title, with its ticket, pull request number and role word before it, such as `ENG-262#73 Reviewer`; a worker's row leaves its role word out, `ENG-262#73`.
At its right end are the model's maker, Anthropic or Z.ai, and the effort its latest reply ran with, each naming itself on hover.
A row with checks or tasks under it shows its arrow in the effort's place while you hover or focus it, while it is open, and always on a phone.
On a narrow sidebar the title cuts from its end.

A pull request's number takes a colour: red while CI fails, orange in review, gray for a draft not reviewed yet or a closed one, green when Ready or merged.

Hover a row's title for its full words, one line each: its name, its title whole, its model, effort and state with what the state holds, **Draft** while its box holds a message you have not sent, its pull request's state in words and, while a worker works or is stuck, the time left to its ETA.

## What runs under a row

A chevron on a row opens what runs under it: its reviewers and testers, its subagents and its background processes.
A process shows only while it runs, by what it does (the description its agent gave it), else by its command.
A finished subagent stays for half an hour.
A row with nothing to list has no chevron.

A stuck reviewer or tester keeps its worker's row closed: the worker's row shows the red alarm instead, with the stuck one named in its tooltip.
