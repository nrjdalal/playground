# Inbox

The Inbox holds what your sessions need from you, and what happened that you should know.
It sits in the half beside every dashboard page: beside your chat on `/dashboard`, and on any other page next to a square for your chat.
It opens there first, under the Inbox square in the small bar at the bottom of that half (on a phone, the bar at the bottom of the page).
On a wide screen that bar hides until you move the pointer near the bottom of the half, or press Tab into it; a short handle marks where it comes back, with a dot while something is new.
Untick **Auto-hide tab bar** at the top of the user menu to keep it shown, with room left under each pane; this browser remembers the choice.

## What it holds

Each item is a card of one of three kinds:

- **Decision**: something a session will do only with your yes, such as a question only you can answer, a Slack message, a ship to main, a new Linear ticket, a delete, a database change or a spend.
  The card shows exactly what happens when you approve it.
- **Draft**: a Slack message or a Linear comment, ready to send in your name.
  The card shows its words, and Open thread shows where it goes.
- **Update**: an outcome you should know, such as a merge, a ship, an alert, a finding or a decision.

The list shows what needs you first, then unread updates, then the rest from the last week, each part newest first.
The Inbox square counts what needs you and came since you last looked at the Inbox, so opening the Inbox clears it.

The Inbox is six rows, each opened or closed by a click:

- **Inbox** holds the list and shows how many it holds, and reads gray when it is empty. It starts open.
- **In progress** holds what you answered in chat, five minutes on, while the session carries it out.
- **Snoozed** holds what you put away until a time.
- **Archived** holds what you set aside for later.
- **Done** holds what is settled.
- **Preview** opens a small browser for active development, there even when the Inbox is empty (see [Preview](#preview)). It starts closed.

Each row but Preview shows how many it holds; a row reads gray while it is empty, and Done reads gray once you have read all of it, though it still opens.
An empty row ends in a small circle, and a row with something in it in an arrow that points down while it is open.
An archived item has its own **Dismiss**, and the check on the Archived row dismisses them all; either sends them to Done.
The check on the Done row marks everything in Done read, and goes once it is all read.
A row's heading stays at the top while you scroll through it.

## Read a card

The line at the top of a card gives its kind, the session that posted it and its id, such as `#i41`, and ends at the right with a check and one time: when it was posted, or once a decision or draft changes (your answer, its delivery, the session closing or editing it), or the session edits an update, when it was updated.
It reads **Updated** in blue when that change came since you last looked at the Inbox.

The icon beside the title is the item's state, drawn like an agent's status icon:

| Icon         | Means                                             |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Orange eye   | It waits on you.                                  |
| Blue spinner | Your answer is being carried out.                 |
| Green check  | It is done, with a green link to where it landed. |
| Red error    | Your answer failed to send.                       |
| Gray circle  | It was denied, discarded, archived or closed.     |
| Dot          | An update you have not read.                      |

## Answer a decision

A session's request in its own words shows as your chat shows a message, so code in it reads highlighted, with a Copy button.
A drawing keeps its lines whole, its text shrinking to fit the card.
A Slack message shows exactly as it will be sent.

- **Approve** tells the session to carry it out.
- **Deny** tells it not to.

Some decisions name what Approve does and what Deny does, one line each.
That card shows the two as choices: press 1 or 2, or click one, then **Send**.

Every open decision and draft ends in an **Add an optional comment** box above its buttons.
Anything you would change goes there: what you type goes to the session with whatever you answer, and the answered card shows it as Your note.

A decision can carry pictures, shown as square previews in a grid of four by four under its words: up to sixteen, or fifteen and a tile saying how many more.
Click one to see it full screen over the page, blurred and dimmed behind it, one at a time, with nothing else on screen.
The arrow keys or a swipe move between them, the arrows going round from the last to the first, and Esc closes the view.
Move the pointer to the bottom edge or the thin pill there, press Tab, or on a phone tap the picture, and a bar rises with the arrows, where you are, such as 2 / 6, the picture's name, Open in new tab and X; the top-right corner shows an X too.
A before and an after picture of the same thing show as one, side by side, Before on the left and After on the right.
When they are the only files, the card shows them at its full width, Before above After.
A bar at the top picks how they show: **Before** or **After** alone, **Side** (the first view) side by side at a divider you drag, or **Slider**, one over the other with a line you drag or move with the left and right arrow keys; the up and down arrow keys move to the picture before or after.
Click a picture to zoom in where you clicked, drag to look around, and click again to zoom out; on a phone a tap shows or hides the bar instead.

Besides pictures, a decision can carry an HTML page, a markdown file, a code file or a PDF, each a tile with its icon and name.
An HTML page that is the only file shows live in the card, with a button in its corner that opens it full screen.
A page runs only inside the view: its own keys work there, the arrows included, so a slide deck moves through its slides, Esc still closes the view, and a link in it opens in a new tab.
An arrow the page does nothing with moves on to the next file.
A markdown file reads like a chat message, code shows highlighted, and a PDF opens in the browser's own viewer.

## Send a draft

A reply to a Slack conversation shows **So far** first: one short line for each message that led up to it, who said it and the gist, oldest first.
Your words follow under **Your reply**.
A Slack decision shows the same lines above the message it sends.

Change the words where they stand, then:

- **Send** sends it as it reads.
  Cmd+Enter or Ctrl+Enter in the box does the same.
- **Draft** saves a Slack message in Slack's Drafts & Sent, for you to finish and send yourself.
  A Linear comment has no Draft, since Linear has no drafts.
- **Discard** drops it.

A draft shows upfront where it will land, or why it has no link yet.

## After you answer

Your answer goes to the session that posted the item, and that session carries it out.
The Dashboard itself sends nothing.

Until the session confirms it, the card reads what is happening, such as Sending in Slack…, Saving a Slack draft… or Being carried out.
Once confirmed, the card links to where it landed, such as Open thread for a Slack message.
A Draft that Slack refused, because that conversation already holds one, reads in gray Draft not saved, with the reason.

When your answer cannot reach the session, the card stays in the list in red, reading Send failed with the reason.
When nothing reached the session yet, **Retry** sends it again.
When part of it may have reached the session already, it reads Send unconfirmed and has no Retry, so it is never sent twice.
A card with no Retry offers Dismiss alone.

## Put an item away

**Snooze**, the zzz button, puts a decision or draft away until a time.
Pick one of the times it offers, each shown with its day and time in IST, or Pick a time… for a day and time of your own.
The item moves to Snoozed and leaves the count until then, then comes back as new.

**Archive**, the archive-box button, sets a decision aside for later: it is neither a yes nor a no.
Whatever is already under way keeps going, and the session does not ask about it again until you bring it up.
The item moves to Archived.

**Mark as read**, the single check at the right end of a card's top line, marks an item you have not read yet as seen and answers nothing: the card stays as it is, still waiting on your answer if it asks one, and the check turns into a gray double check.

**Dismiss** moves an update, or a decision or draft you have answered, a reply in chat included, to Done at once.
An update you dismiss counts as read.
**Mark all as read**, the check at the right of the first row, marks everything on screen you have not read as seen, answering nothing; each stays in the list as it is, and the check turns into a gray double check, All read.

An item you answered moves to Done as soon as the session that posted it says it is done, unless you archived it.
An answer still being carried out, or an update you read, moves to Done by itself after ten minutes.

## Preview

Under Done sits **Preview**, the last row, closed until you open it; clicking Playground at the top of the sidebar opens it too.
Opened, it turns the rest of the pane into a small browser for active development.
Its list shows every worktree of this app and of each project a running session works in, grouped by project, each by its branch and the session working in it.
Pick a running one, or one of the 8 newest pages from Previews, or type an address.
It loads only a `.localhost` address or a Previews page, and it remembers the last address you opened.
Reload and Open in a new tab sit at the end of its address bar: a new tab opens the worktree's own app, where it works with its own sign-in.

Pick a stopped one and click **Start** to run it as a Vibeground: its branch as it stands, on a copy of your data, so you see real data while its database changes stay in the copy.
It opens here once it is up, a minute or so; until then it reads Starting, with a **Stop** of its own.
**Stop**, in the bar while a Vibeground is open, stops it and drops its copy; the worktree stays.
While your Mac is busy only one Vibeground runs at a time, and none when it is very busy.

## Chat about it

Click **Chat about it**, the button at the right end of a card, to talk about it.
Your chat opens with Replying to #i41 above the box, and the message you send carries the id.
Typing `re #i41` anywhere in a message does the same.

A reply counts as acting on the item.
It leaves the list and the count at once, and its card reads You replied, with your words, and that the session is on it, until the session closes it.

## While you look

The list holds still while you read it.
An item that arrives meanwhile waits behind a pill on the line above the list, such as **1 new update**.
Click it to put what waits at the top.

When cards move anyway, the buttons on each card that moved pause for 1 second, so a click meant for another card lands on none.

A session that posted a decision or draft you have not acted on reads Waiting on your inbox in the sidebar while it is idle, with an orange eye (see [Agents](/docs/agents)).
